# Assessment Task 2

## Project Documentation

### Requirements Outline

#### The Need
Waking up at 2:00 AM to use the bathroom or other activites and either stubbing your toe in the dark or blinding yourself by turning on the bright overhead lights

#### The Proposed Solution
We will design an Plug-in Nightlight system. You plug these directly into any standard wall outlet in your hallway, kitchen, or bathroom. 

How it works      : The built-in light sensor detects if the sun is up or the overhead lights are on; if it is bright, the light stays completely asleep. The moment the room goes dark, the motion sensor activates. If you walk past, it turns on a gentle, downward-facing glow for about 30 seconds, then turns off automatically
